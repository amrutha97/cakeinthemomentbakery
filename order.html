How to Order

Ordering made simple

We take a limited number of custom orders each week so every cake gets our full attention.

Send an inquiry

DM us on Instagram or use the inquiry form. Include your date, servings, flavors and inspiration photos.

Get your quote

We'll confirm availability within 1–2 days and send a custom quote with design notes.

Secure your date

A 50% non-refundable deposit reserves your spot. The balance is due before pickup.

Pick up & celebrate

Collect your cake at your scheduled time in Bothell. We'll include care and transport tips.

Lead time

We recommend booking 2–3 weeks in advance, and 1–2 months for weddings and holidays. Last-minute? Message us. We'll try!

Pickup & delivery

Pickup in Bothell by appointment. Local delivery within the Greater Seattle area may be available for an added fee.

Payment

We accept common payment apps and cash. Details are included with your quote and invoice.

FAQ

Good to know

How far in advance should I order?

2–3 weeks is ideal for most cakes. Popular weekends and holidays fill quickly, so earlier is always better. For weddings, reach out 1–2 months ahead.

How much does a custom cake cost?

Pricing depends on size, number of tiers and design complexity. Share your servings and inspiration photos and we'll send a personalized quote.

Can you recreate a cake I saw online?

We love inspiration photos! We'll use them as a starting point and put our own touch on the design so it's uniquely yours.

Do you offer allergy-friendly options?

Ask us about eggless and other dietary options. Please note all cakes are made in a home kitchen that also handles wheat, dairy, eggs, soy and nuts, so we cannot guarantee any product is allergen-free.

How should I transport and store my cake?

Carry the box flat on the floor of your car (not on a seat), keep the A/C on, and refrigerate until about 1–2 hours before serving for the best texture.

What is your cancellation policy?

Deposits are non-refundable but can be applied to a future order if you reschedule at least 7 days before your pickup date.

Do you deliver?

Pickup is in Bothell. Delivery may be available in the Greater Seattle area for an additional fee. Just mention it in your inquiry.
