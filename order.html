How to Order

Ordering Made Simple

We take a limited number of custom orders each week so every cake gets our full attention.

Send an Inquiry

DM us on Instagram or use the inquiry form. Include your date, servings, flavors and inspiration photos.

Get Your Quote

We'll reply within 24 hours to confirm availability and send a custom quote with design notes.

Secure Your Date

A 50% non-refundable deposit reserves your spot. The balance is due before pickup.

Pick Up & Celebrate

Collect your cake at your scheduled time in Bothell. We'll include care and transport tips.

Lead Time

A minimum of 1 week notice is required, and the earlier the better. Need it sooner? Message us. Rush orders may be possible depending on availability, with a late fee.

Pickup

All orders are picked up in Bothell. We'll coordinate pickup details closer to your date and are happy to be flexible on timing.

Payment

We accept Venmo, Zelle and tap to pay. Payment details are included with your quote and invoice.

FAQ

Good to Know

How far in advance should I order?

As much advance notice as possible is always best, since popular weekends and holidays fill up quickly. We require a minimum of 1 week notice for all orders. If you need something in less than a week, reach out anyway! We may be able to accommodate your order depending on availability, and a late fee will apply.

How much does a custom cake cost?

Pricing depends on size, number of tiers and design complexity. Share your servings and inspiration photos and we'll send a personalized quote.

Can you recreate a cake I saw online?

We love inspiration photos! We'll use them as a starting point and put our own touch on the design so it's uniquely yours.

Do you offer allergy-friendly options?

Yes! Ask us about eggless options and other dietary restrictions, and we'll do our best to accommodate. Please note: we are a home bakery, and our kitchen regularly handles wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, nuts and other common allergens. We take every precaution we can, but we cannot guarantee that any product is completely allergen-free. If you or your guests have a severe allergy, please use your best judgment.

How should I transport and store my cake?

Carry the box flat on the floor of your car (not on a seat) and keep the A/C on. Store your cake in the refrigerator until it's time to serve. For buttercream cakes, take the cake out of the fridge 30 minutes to 1 hour before serving so the frosting can come to room temperature for the best taste and texture.

What is your cancellation policy?

All orders are confirmed with a 50% non-refundable deposit. If an order is cancelled, the deposit will not be returned.

Do you deliver?

Unfortunately, we don't offer delivery at this time. All orders are picked up in Bothell. We'll coordinate pickup details with you closer to your date, and we're happy to be flexible on timing.
