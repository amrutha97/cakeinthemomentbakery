Our story

A Home Bakery Built on Celebrating the Little Moments

Cake in the Moment began in 2025 in a home kitchen in Bothell, with a simple idea: the cake should feel as special as the person it's for.

Every order is baked fresh from scratch with real butter, quality chocolate and seasonal fruit, then decorated by hand. Because we take a limited number of orders each week, each cake gets the time and care it deserves.
